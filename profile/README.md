## It starts with an idea

Alileus is a venture studio. Some projects start with something we want to build ourselves. Others come from founders who bring us an idea and want to work on it together.

## From idea to product

We get involved in the building, from an early version to a live product:

- Develop the idea into something people can try.
- Share the work and its progress in our public portfolio.
- Form a team around the project to take it forward.

## Working together

You can come to us with an idea, or find a project you want to be part of.

- **Join a team.** [Browse the portfolio](https://www.alile.us/en/t) and apply to a venture that fits your interests and skills.
- **Bring an idea.** [Tell us what you want to build](https://www.alile.us/en/propose). If it's a fit, we'll meet and work out the next step.
- **Meet the community.** Our [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/alileus) is where you can find the people behind the work.
